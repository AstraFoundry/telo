## Design Context

### Users

Telo serves desktop Telegram users who read and write conversations for sustained periods and want an AI assistant beside the active chat. They need fast navigation, dense but legible information, reliable keyboard and pointer interaction, and confidence that private credentials and message context stay contained on their device.

### Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and privacy-conscious. The interface should feel native to the desktop, precise under frequent use, and quietly polished rather than decorative or attention-seeking.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a refined desktop-workspace aesthetic inspired by Telegram Desktop and platform-native macOS, Windows, and Linux conventions. Support light and dark themes through the existing semantic tokens. Preserve Telo's restrained surfaces, compact toolbars, rounded conversation column, and purposeful motion. Avoid generic dashboard cards, ornamental gradients, excessive glass effects, and mobile patterns transplanted unchanged onto desktop.

### Design Principles

1. Respect platform chrome and system-defined safe areas at every window size.
2. Keep high-frequency chat actions immediate, stable, and spatially consistent.
3. Adapt the information architecture when the window narrows instead of merely shrinking controls.
4. Use semantic tokens, shared primitives, and one reusable layout rule for repeated behavior.
5. Preserve accessibility, keyboard operation, reduced motion, and comfortable pointer targets as first-class behavior.
